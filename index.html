FAQs

Questions Parents Ask Us

Which courses are offered for 2027–28?

Intermediate MPC (with IIT-JEE or Design), BiPC + NEET, MEC + CLAT, CEC + CA Foundation, ACE, IPMAT & CUET, and CBSE 11th & 12th with integrated commerce coaching.

How does the admission process work?

Submit the enquiry form, take a short admission exam that places your child in the right stream and batch, and then confirm enrolment.

Do you offer hostel facilities?

Yes. We offer day scholar, semi-residential and residential options, with separate boys' and girls' hostel blocks and hygienic veg & non-veg catering.

Where is the campus and is transport available?

The 3.5-acre campus is at ORR Exit 16, Rallaguda Road, Shamshabad, Hyderabad. AC buses with CCTV cover major routes.

Is the college recognised?

Yes. Accomplish Junior College is recognised by the Telangana Board of Intermediate Education (TGBIE).
